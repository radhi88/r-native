# 12 Archive — Archive, Don't Delete

## The Rule
**Never delete a note. Archive it.**

When a note is no longer active — a finished project, an outdated workflow, a rejected idea, a superseded decision — move it here instead of deleting it.

Why:
- Old notes keep their `[[links]]`, so the knowledge graph stays intact.
- "Dead" ideas often come back alive months later with new context.
- Past decisions and their reasoning are the company's institutional memory — deleting them deletes the lessons.

## القاعدة بالعربي
**لا تحذف أبدًا — أرشِف.**
أي ملاحظة انتهى دورها (مشروع اكتمل، فكرة رُفضت، إجراء تغيّر) انقلها إلى هذا المجلد بدل حذفها. الروابط تبقى حيّة، والأفكار الميتة كثيرًا ما تعود، وقرارات الماضي هي ذاكرة الشركة ودروسها.

## How to Archive
1. Move the note (or the whole project folder) into "12 Archive".
2. Optionally add a line at the top: `Archived: YYYY-MM-DD — reason`.
3. Do not strip its links or tags — they are the memory.
