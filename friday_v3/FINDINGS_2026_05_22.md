## Critical Bug Found & Fixed

All v3 code hardcoded `POINT = 0.01`, but MT5 reports `point = 0.001` for
XAUUSDm (3-digit pricing). Every distance calculation was off by 10×.

**Fixed**: `dip_detector.py` now auto-reads `mt5.symbol_info(symbol).point`.
**Verified**: corrected backtests confirm the underlying problem isn't the bug —
the strategy itself has no edge.

## Three Mathematical Findings

### 1. Spread eats every potential profit

- Spread: 308 actual points = **$0.308 per round-trip** at 0.01 lot
- To gain $0.03 of bid: market must move **+338 points** (30 target + 308 spread)
- To lose $0.30 of bid: market only needs to move **-60 points** from entry
- **Asymmetric ruin guaranteed**

### 2. 91% of "dip" signals are noise

Of 141 high-quality dip signals (score≥70, RSI<30, drop≥1.5×ATR):
- 5.67% reversed enough to hit +500pt bid target (BUY)
- 2.84% continued enough to hit -500pt ask target (SELL)
- **91.5% chopped sideways within ±300pt SL band**

### 3. M5 SL of 300pt is below typical ATR

- M5 ATR on gold = $2-5 (2,000-5,000 points)
- Our $0.30 SL = 300 points
- Single bar routinely blows through this — no edge possible

## Real Working Constraints

For this account ($50, XAUUSDm, 308pt spread), mechanical scalping needs ALL of:

1. **Target ≥ spread × 3** = at least $1.00 per win (≈1,000pt move)
2. **SL ≥ ATR × 2** = at least $4.00 per loss (4,000pt move) → blows account
3. **WR > 80%** to overcome the unfavorable R:R

These constraints conflict. No tuning of M1/M5 dip strategy can satisfy them.

## What Could Actually Work

Three structurally different paths:

### A. Lower-spread symbol/account
- Switch to **XAUUSD** (non-"m" suffix, typically 30-50pt spread)
- Or open **Exness Raw/Zero** account (5-10pt spread + commission)
- Spread cost drops 6-10× → scalping math becomes possible

### B. Higher timeframe + bigger targets
- Trade **H1 / H4** with ATR-scaled targets ($3-5 wins, $1.5 SL)
- Win rate ~50% becomes acceptable with R:R 2:1
- ~3-8 trades per week instead of dozens per day

### C. News event straddle only
- Spread doesn't matter when market moves 1,000pt in 60 seconds
- News parser **already fixed** (4 high-impact events found this week)
- This was always the only strategy that mathematically survives 308pt spread
